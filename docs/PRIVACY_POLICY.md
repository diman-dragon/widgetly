# Privacy Policy — Widget Studio

_Last updated: <DATE>_

Widget Studio ("the app") lets you build a home-screen widget with a clock, date, weather, alarm and notifications.

## Data we collect
We do not run servers, do not require an account and do not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no advertising and no analytics.

## Data stored on your device
Widget designs, settings and a cached weather reading are stored locally in the app's private storage. Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Network requests
To show weather the app sends the latitude/longitude of the city you selected to the weather service you chose (Open-Meteo, https://open-meteo.com/, or MET Norway, https://api.met.no/). When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to the Open-Meteo geocoding service. These providers receive your IP address as part of any internet request and handle it under their own policies. No other data is sent.

## Notification access
If you enable it in system settings, the app reads the list of active notifications on your device only to display their count and titles inside your widget. This information is processed on the device, is not transmitted anywhere and can be disabled at any time in Settings → Notifications → Notification access.

## Children
The app is not directed to children under 13.
